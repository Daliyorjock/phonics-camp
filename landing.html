The Problem

Why Traditional Methods Fail

Memorize → forget → re-memorize. The rote learning loop is broken. Most language apps are visual-first, gamified but fragmented, and lack authentic rhythm and prosody.

Rote Memorization

Drill-and-repeat creates frustration, not retention. The forgetting curve wins every time without proper spaced repetition.

Visual-Only Learning

Flashcards and translation pairs ignore the auditory cortex — the brain region that evolved for language acquisition.

Fragmented Content

Learning disconnected words without musical context fails to build the phonetic and prosodic patterns that define fluency.

💡

Key insight: Humans naturally acquire language through rhythm and repetition — the same way infants learn their mother tongue. Not through word lists, but through musical patterns that embed themselves in memory.

The Method

The 4-Stage Learning Loop

A complete cycle from listening to mastery — designed so you never feel like you're "studying."

1

Hear First

~2.5 seconds · 0.6x speed · Pure voice

Crystal-clear human audio reads the target phrase. You know exactly what you're about to learn — no mystery, no anxiety.

2

Earworm Jingle

~18 seconds · 6 music loops · Progressive voice mix

Loops 1-2: pure bassline establishes rhythm. Loops 3-4: human voice enters subtly at 30%. Loops 5-6: voice becomes clear and rhythm-synced. Your brain treats it as music — but it's acquiring language patterns.

3

Sound Bridge

Syllable-level color mapping · Tap to hear each sound

See each syllable color-coded by vowel/consonant, with stress markers. Click any syllable to hear it spoken. Normal (1.0x) and slow (0.55x) playback. Bridges the gap between hearing and spelling.

4

Progressive Recall

Recognition → Fill-in-blank → Full recall with Game Drop

Three tiers of increasing difficulty. You only face challenges you're ready for. The final tier transforms spelling into a micro-game — characters drop from the top, hit the right key to score.

Design Philosophy

Anti-Textbook Design

Four principles that flip traditional language education on its head.

🧠

Implicit Statistical Learning

Your brain auto-extracts language patterns through repeated exposure — no explicit teaching required.

🎹

Musical Texture Encoding

Language embedded in music activates both auditory and motor cortex — dual-channel memory encoding.

Core Features

6 Engines, One Experience

Each component is independently designed, seamlessly integrated.

Earworm Music Engine

Vector synthesizer with 16-step bass sequencer. 6-loop progressive voice mixing via Web Audio API. 4 preset timbres: mellow, pluck, pad, bass.

Sound Bridge

Syllable-level color decomposition. Vowels in blue, consonants in pink, stress marked with ▼. Tap any syllable to hear it spoken.

Progressive Recall Testing

Three-tier difficulty: Recognition → Cloze → Full Recall. Real-time feedback. Errors trigger intelligent correction, not punishment.

Leitner Box SRS

5-level spaced repetition: L0 (new) → L1 → L2(+1d) → L3(+3d) → L4(+7d) → Mastered. localStorage persistence, survives refresh.

Game Drop

Characters fall from the top. 9-second countdown. Hit the right key to score with audio feedback. Spelling practice becomes a micro-game.

Human Voice Fusion

Pre-generated MP3 via Microsoft en-US-AriaNeural (highest-rated English voice). Base64 embedded. Variable-speed playback (0.55x–1.0x).
